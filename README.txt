IMPORTANT BEFORE SHARING/PUBLISHING
- In contact.html, replace hello@REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-EMAIL.example with an email address you control. Search for the placeholder and replace it in the mailto: link.
- The quote form prepares a draft in the visitor's email app; it does not automatically send or store submissions. A real backend/form service is needed for submissions without an email app.
- Apex Labs name/domain availability has not been verified. A public URL requires a registered available domain and hosting/deployment.
- Portfolio items are explicitly demo concepts. Reviews are not fabricated; add only genuine reviews with permission.
- Prices are proposed introductory ranges. Clearly agree on deliverables, revision limits, timeline, domain/hosting costs, and payment terms before starting client work.
